Privacy Policy & User Data Guidelines

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

At Viralify ("we", "our", or "us"), accessible from https://socialposting-eight.vercel.app, we prioritize the privacy and security of our users' personal data and social media authorizations.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Viralify to schedule and publish video content, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and authentication identifiers provided during sign-in.
  • Social Platform Tokens: OAuth 2.0 access tokens, refresh tokens, page access tokens, and account IDs provided by connected platforms (including Google/YouTube, Meta/Facebook/Instagram, and TikTok).
  • Content Data: Video titles, descriptions, hashtags, thumbnail previews, and schedule timestamps created within the Composer module.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to deliver social media scheduling and publishing functionality:

  • To authenticate your session and verify authorization across connected platforms.
  • To publish video content and metadata to target social media platforms upon your explicit request.
  • To retrieve channel metrics (e.g. subscriber count, follower count, view count) for your personal dashboard display.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement strict security measures to protect your credentials:

  • All OAuth access tokens are stored securely in backend server databases (Firebase Firestore) and are never exposed to browser client scripts.
  • All network requests between Viralify, our servers, and third-party APIs use HTTPS encryption.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or social media credentials to third parties.

4. User Data Deletion Instructions

You have full control over your connected accounts and stored data. You can request deletion of your data at any time:

  • In-App Disconnect: Go to the Accounts section inside Viralify and click Disconnect on any connected platform card. This instantly revokes and deletes all associated OAuth tokens from our database.
  • Meta / Facebook Data Removal: You can remove Viralify's access directly via your Facebook Account:
    1. Go to your Facebook Profile's Settings & Privacy > Settings.
    2. Select Business Integrations or Apps and Websites.
    3. Find Viralify and click Remove.
  • Complete Account & Data Wipe Request: If you wish to delete your entire Viralify account and all stored history, please contact our support team at support@viralify.app. All associated records will be deleted within 48 hours.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at support@viralify.app.
